# Product

## Vision

Zury is a personal academic companion that helps students understand their day,
make realistic plans and keep moving when connectivity is unreliable.

It is an academic operating system, not a chatbot and not a developer tool. The
technology should recede into the background while the student's priorities,
time and progress remain visible.

## Positioning

Zury brings planning, study context and timely guidance into one calm place. It
turns scattered academic responsibilities into a clear next step.

Short pitch:

> Your day, your studies and your next move, together in one calm place.

## Audience

Primary users are university students, beginning with students in Africa who
often work across mobile and desktop with expensive or unreliable connectivity.

They are consumers, not system operators. They should not need to understand
models, agents, tools, integrations or synchronization architecture to benefit
from Zury.

## Core Needs

- Know what matters today and this week
- Build a realistic study plan around existing commitments
- Capture and revisit useful academic context
- Take action without moving between many apps
- Continue working when internet access is slow or unavailable
- Trust which information is current, saved or waiting to update

## Product Principles

### Today first

Open with what is timely and actionable. The dashboard is a personal daily
briefing, not a collection of widgets.

### Outcomes before conversation

Prefer clear plans, recommendations and one-tap actions. Conversation is
available when useful, but it is not the product's default shape.

### Minimal cognitive load

Show fewer, better choices. Use progressive disclosure and preserve the
student's focus.

### Offline with dignity

Offline mode is a first-class experience, not a degraded error state. Clearly
label saved information and queue safe actions without making users manage the
sync process.

### Honest intelligence

Never imply access to information Zury does not have. Explain uncertainty,
cached data and unavailable connections in plain language.

### Personal over performative

Zury should feel attentive and adaptable without gamifying anxiety. Avoid
productivity scores, guilt, streak pressure and celebratory noise for routine
work.

### Premium but accessible

High-end design means considered, fast and reliable, not visually extravagant
or financially exclusionary. The experience must remain usable on modest
devices and networks.

## Experience Model

The main experience is organized around four consumer concepts:

- **Today:** priorities, schedule and the most useful next action
- **Plan:** calendar and realistic allocation of work
- **Study:** notes, study sessions and academic context
- **Ask:** a focused way to clarify, find or act when direct UI is insufficient

Technical features map into these concepts instead of becoming top-level
destinations. For example, GitHub may provide project context, but users should
see the project or assignment, not a developer integration dashboard.

## Product Language

Prefer:

- Today
- Your week
- Study session
- Saved offline
- Connected apps
- Up to date
- Needs your attention

Avoid in primary product surfaces:

- Agent
- Tool call
- Model
- Sync queue
- Cache
- Repository
- API
- Database

Technical terms may appear in developer documentation, diagnostics or advanced
settings when genuinely necessary.

## Scope Guardrails

- Do not turn the dashboard into analytics or a widget marketplace.
- Do not make chat the only way to complete common tasks.
- Do not foreground GitHub or coding workflows in the brand.
- Do not expose infrastructure status as product navigation.
- Do not add social, competitive or streak mechanics without user research.
