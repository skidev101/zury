# Demo Script

## Story

The demo follows one student preparing for a demanding week. Lead with clarity
and confidence, not with AI or technical architecture.

## Flow

1. Sign in and arrive at a calm personal briefing for today.
2. Show the next commitment and the one item that needs attention.
3. Open "Your week" and see classes, deadlines and available study time in one
   composed view.
4. Ask Zury to make room for an upcoming exam.
5. Review the proposed study plan and accept it with one clear action.
6. Turn off connectivity and continue viewing the saved schedule and notes.
7. Make a safe change while offline and show "Will update when you're online."
8. Restore connectivity and let the interface quietly return to "Up to date."

## Presentation Rules

- Describe benefits in student language.
- Keep technical implementation invisible unless the audience asks.
- Do not lead with chat, a model name, GitHub or tool execution.
- Do not present offline behavior as an error recovery trick; it is normal Zury
  behavior.
- Use real-looking academic content and avoid fake productivity analytics.

## Closing Message

Zury gives students a clear next move, even when the internet cannot.
