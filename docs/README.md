# Zury

Zury is an offline-first personal academic companion built for university
students. It brings schedules, study context and useful guidance into one calm,
high-quality consumer experience.

## Product Promise

> Your day, your studies and your next move, together in one calm place.

Zury begins with students in Africa, where unreliable or expensive connectivity
should not prevent someone from planning, studying or accessing their latest
information.

## Core Principles

- Today first
- Offline with dignity
- Honest intelligence
- Recommendations over unnecessary conversation
- Minimal cognitive load
- Premium consumer experience
- Technology that stays in the background

## Experience

Zury opens as a personal daily briefing, not an admin dashboard. It helps a
student see what matters, make a realistic plan and act with as little friction
as possible. When connectivity changes, Zury remains useful and explains the
state of information in simple, reassuring language.
